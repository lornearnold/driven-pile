## Known differences from the research code, by design

- No rounding of the per-foot formula capacity to whole kips.
- `Pile.octagonal(24, 150)` computes the true octagon area (3.3137 ft^2); the research code and the tests use the nominal 477 in^2 (3.3125 ft^2).
- `MonteCarloOptions.ci_target` defaults to 0.02 as the paper states; the research runs used 0.05.
- No minimum log length (Lorne, 2026-10-23). The research code skipped logs with fewer than 20 valid feet, a cutoff with no recorded rationale; `prepare_target` now rejects only a log with no valid feet, and the smoothing window shrinks to fit short logs. All 15 benchmark logs exceed 20 feet, so the regression results are unchanged.
- The top-segment point estimate is the lowest admissible friction angle, not the noise-broken argmin of a flat RMS curve.
- Per-pile mudline overrides and the Savitzky-Golay settings are inputs (`SoilProfile.mudline`, `ModelOptions.smoothing_*`), not constants.
- The paper says the interface friction angle equals phi' (Eq. 5); the research code and the package use 0.8 phi' in the Coyle and Castello branch (`cc_delta_ratio`). Reconciling this is out of scope for now (Lorne, 2026-10-23).
